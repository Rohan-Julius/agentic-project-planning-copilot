# Estimation Guidance

Story points are a relative measure of effort and uncertainty, not hours. Use the modified
Fibonacci scale: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13.

- 1-2 points: a well-understood change to existing, familiar code.
- 3-5 points: a new capability with a few unknowns, but no new architecture.
- 8 points: touches multiple components or requires new infrastructure.
- 13 points: should usually be split into smaller stories before sprint planning.

Estimates are always suggestions for planning purposes. Never present a story-point total
as a guaranteed delivery commitment to stakeholders — actual delivery depends on team
capacity, unknowns discovered during implementation, and competing priorities.
