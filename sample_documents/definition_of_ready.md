# Definition of Ready

A user story is ready for sprint planning when all of the following are true:

1. The story follows the "As a [persona], I want [capability], so that [business benefit]"
   format.
2. Acceptance criteria are written in Given/When/Then format and cover at least the primary
   success path.
3. The story is small enough to complete within a single sprint (typically 1-8 story points).
4. Dependencies on other stories or external systems are identified and either resolved or
   explicitly tracked.
5. The story has been reviewed by at least one other team member for clarity.
6. Any UI/UX mockups or API contracts the story depends on are linked or attached.
7. The product owner has confirmed the business value statement is accurate.
